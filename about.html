Nice to meet you!

⚚ | About Me

An Interaction designer with a bad habit of overthinking user flows.

Hello, it’s nice to meet you, I’m Emma! I’m an artist turned Interaction Design student at Sheridan College, I am fascinated by the relationship between people, science, history, mythology, technology, and visual storytelling. Learning is my favourite past-time and I continue to educate myself with everyday.

Ancient civilizations around the world, had different ways they mapped the stars. T hey interpreted patterns into the constellations to represent their stories and beliefs. I approach design the same way, by researching, storytelling, communication and understanding what users need. My goal as a person and designer is to make the world better everyday. I wish to create accessible experiences with a purpose. How might we transform these complex situations into a digestible solution?
