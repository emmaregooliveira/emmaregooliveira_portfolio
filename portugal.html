01 | OVERVIEW

Portugal: Travel Back in Time is an interactive website featuring the timeline of the history of Portugal, famous Portuguese individuals, an interactive chat bot and resources. Narrative was a big component for the history aspects, which helped me to properly create a pleasing and accessible experience. The goal was to educate users about Portugal's beautiful culture.

02 | PROBLEM

“How might we assist English‑speaking 2nd and 3rd generation Portuguese‑North Americans explore how Portugal has changed over time , by letting them interact with live historical and demographic data and key events in an accessible English‑language interface.”

03 | TESTING & RESEARCH

Historical research is scattered across many websites and presented in text-heavy formats, making it less enjoyable for young audiences.

A 2024 study by Haliti-Sylaj found that frequent short-form video consumption was associated with reduced attention spans and poor academic performance.

This information highlights the importance of educational content being engaging and interactive. (Haliti-Sylaj and Sadiku 64)

Historical Research

Before starting the project, I explored Portugal's historical timeline and considered how this information could be transformed into a digital interface.

API Exploration

As this was my first experience using APIs, I investigated how they function and how historical information could be retrieved and presented.

Design Direction

The resulting design direction combines a structured information architecture, traditional Portuguese-inspired colours, expressive typography, and contemporary interaction patterns. Together, these decisions established a foundation for a website intended to make historical exploration more approachable.

05 | DRAFTS & ITERATIONS

From initial sketches to refined interfaces, each iteration helped me shape the website's structure, navigation, and visual identity.

Low Fidelity

I started the process by creating low-fid wireframes in Figma. I wanted to explore how I could explore all of Portugal's information I wanted to include.

Focusing mainly on placement, navigation, and visual hierarchy. I decided to focus on colours and typography next.

These early wireframes helped me figure out what needed improvement before moving on.

Mid-High Fidelity

As I moved into mid-high fidelity designs, I tried out different colour palettes and typography, which were inspired by Portugal's flag.

Using Figma, I decided where I would place the information. I wanted to create a vintage/historical appearance while the site stayed readable.

Final High Fidelity

This was my final choice. Created in Figma and coded in Phoenix Code. I was very satisfied with the simplicity of these choices.

The images in my mid-high fidelity wireframes were very complicated to implement. So I came back to FIGMA and simplified my choices.

Although I didn't add colours here, I decided to continue with the colours I had used in my previous wireframes decision were ideal.

Reflection on Changes

Looking back at my earlier wireframes, I was trying to include way too much information and make the website complicated, more than it needed to be.

As I moved onto high-fidelity designs, I realized that my original ideas weren't practical and too complicated to develop. I simplified the layouts, removed unnecessary elements, and changed the website into one page with some pop-ups.

The final design ended up being very different from my first ideas, I think these changes made the website easier to navigate while keeping the aesthetic I wanted.

06 | FINAL PRODUCT

This is the final Portugal: Travel Back in Time website design. The finished experience contains historical content, interactivity, simple navigation, and imagery of Portugal.
